About the data

These charts use published survey aggregates. Media categories can overlap, and questions and respondent bases differ between sources. The shared slider aligns survey years; it does not combine the surveys.

  • How we watch and listen to content data tables: viewing by age, QF4.
  • Television & Media Survey 2025 summary report: historical viewing hours, C2, and social-platform measures, SOCIAL1 and SOCIAL2.
